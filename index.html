One-page business websites, built to work on a phone

Most people find a local business on a phone, and a great many small business sites still do not work properly on one — text too small to read, a page that slides sideways, a phone number that is not tappable. One page built properly beats five pages nobody can use.

What you get

One page with what you do, where you work, your hours and your contact details, written plainly and laid out to be read on a phone first.

Hosting is included, and the page stays plain and fast — no trackers and no cookie banner for your customers to dismiss.

How it goes

You tell us about the business and send whatever photos you already have. We build the page and send you a link to look at before anything goes public.

Changes after that are part of the job, not an extra call-out.

Price

Every job is a different size, so we quote rather than post a number we would have to take back. Ask for a quote and you get a real one in writing.

The Phone Test

A check of a business website at the width of an actual phone, with a written report. It measures whether the page slides sideways, whether any text is too small to read comfortably, whether the connection is secure, and whether the basics a customer looks for are on the page at all. You get the findings in writing and in plain language — not a score out of a hundred with nothing behind it.

It works on your own site, or on one you are thinking of replacing.

Privacy policy

Last updated 9 September 2026.

What this website collects

Nothing. This site is a set of static pages. It has no contact form, no login, no analytics and no cookies. If you email us, we receive what you choose to send us and nothing more.

What we collect when you do business with us

  • Your name, phone number, email address and the address of the property in question
  • Notes about the property and our conversations, so we can follow up accurately
  • Documents you choose to send us, such as a contract or an estimate

How we use it

Only to do the work you contacted us about: quoting a job, evaluating a property, arranging a sale, or following up on one of those. We keep records as long as needed for that work and for our legal and tax obligations.

Who we share it with

Only the people needed to complete a transaction — for example a title company, closing attorney or a buyer for a property you have agreed to sell. We do not sell your information, and we do not share phone numbers or contact details with third parties for their own marketing. Mobile information is never shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

Your choices

You can ask us what we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it, by emailing luxucleen@gmail.com. You can stop text messages at any time by replying STOP.
